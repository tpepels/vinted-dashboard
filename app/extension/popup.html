How automatic updates work

When Chrome is running, the bridge checks Vinted every two hours. It opens a temporary background tab, synchronizes your signed-in account and closes the tab. Vinted does not need to be open. Failed checks can retry at the next interval; successful checks are spaced at least two hours apart. You can also check manually at any time.

After a Vinted access limit, automatic checking pauses for 24 hours. You can still click Check Vinted for updates manually, although Vinted may reject the request.

It sends inventory and sales information to your dashboard. Your password, raw cookies and unrelated notifications are not sent.
