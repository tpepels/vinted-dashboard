How automatic updates work

Automatic checks run at most once every two hours while Vinted is open and inactive. Use Check Vinted for updates whenever you want a manual check. The bridge never opens or reloads Vinted tabs.

After a Vinted access limit, checking pauses for 24 hours.

It sends inventory and sales information to your dashboard. Your password, raw cookies and unrelated notifications are not sent.
