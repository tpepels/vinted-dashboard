PRIVACY

Privacy policy draft

This beta stores account, inventory, marketplace listing, order and analytics data needed to provide the reseller dashboard.

The Chrome companion sends the listing, order and analytics data required by the product from the user's signed-in Vinted tab. It does not send Vinted passwords or raw cookie values to the dashboard.

Extension credentials are revocable. Connector secrets stored by the hosted application are encrypted at rest. Workspace data can be exported from Settings.

Before a public launch this draft must be replaced with the final controller identity, retention periods, subprocessors, contact details and jurisdiction-specific disclosures.
